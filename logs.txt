#### Complete ICT Technician Starter Toolkit

This is a practical kit for a school ICT office, ICT help desk, computer lab, or small organization.

* RJ45 connectors (Cat5e/Cat6)
* RJ45 crimping tool
* Network cable tester
* Punch-down tool
* Wire stripper
* Ethernet cables (various lengths)
* RJ45 couplers
* Cable ties and Velcro straps
* USB-to-Ethernet adapter
* Wi-Fi USB adapter
* Small network switch (5-port or 8-port)
* Spare router or access point
